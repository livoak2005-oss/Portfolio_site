Red Rook Press

Founded in Fall 2022, Red Rook Press is an student-run publishing house within the University of Alabama. The press's mission is to bring diverse voices and stories to publishing from college student authours from around the world.After a year and a half of internal planning, the Special Projects Department is now an established within Red Rook Press. This spring, the team produced their first publication "Bughouse" a zine featuring a collection of short stories and art by university students. Showcased this April in the annual Red Rook Press launch party, the zine is not yet available for purchase online. about what I do, it can take me anywhere if I want to go bad enough.

Red Rook Press 2026 Launch Party

The Red Rook Press 2026 Launch Party was held on April 18th, 2024. The event celebrated the launch of the new Special Projects Department and the release of their first publication, Bughouse. Guests enjoyed live readings from featured authors and artists, followed by a Q&A session with the creative team.
