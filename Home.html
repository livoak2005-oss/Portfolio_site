I am a Public Relations major with a focus on client work and project management. In and out of the classroom I have grown my skills in content strategy and marketing. I love partnering with local non-profits to provide her skills, both through PRSSA and for solo volunteer work in the off seasons. I came to school with an array of passions, and a personal crisis on not knowing where to channel them. Through public relations, I have found a work that is MEANT to put me anywhere. That is my favorite part about what I do, it can take me anywhere if I want to go bad enough.

Outside of school, my hobbies include (but are not limited to) linocut printmaking, sewing, and beadwork. I write in array of creative fiction styles ranging from poems to playwriting. My work Gladiolus Rising was produced by the Glenbard North theatre company in the spring of 2023. I love sharing my artistic work whenever I can. When I can't however, I take pride in showcasing the work of others through my work in PR.
