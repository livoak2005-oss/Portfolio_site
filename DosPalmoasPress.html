Dos Palomas Press

I worked as a paid consultant on the brand messaging for the book tour of "Article 9", the debut novel of independent author Daniel Holub. I designed a digital presentation and accompanying speaking points to align with author’s messaging goals. I also established the current brand kit for Dos Palomas Press to utilize in future promotional materials.

NIn 2025, "Article 9" was named as a finalist for the "IAN Book of the Year" awards under the political fiction/government category.
