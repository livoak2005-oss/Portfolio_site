Volunteer Work

As summer volunteer work, I reached out to the Crisis Services of North Alabama. I was asked to update a presentation for educational outreach geared toward businesses in the surrounding Huntsville area. I compiled updated statistics on domestic violence and its negative impacts on the workplace. I then created an updated outreach presentation. Adhering to the assigned visual and copy requirements but implementing recolored graphics in the organization's brand colors.
