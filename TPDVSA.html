GIRLS NIGHT IN

Excluding Doing More with Less, I executed three monthly internal shelter events known as "Girls Night In". Hosted for current shelter residents, I created activity-based events for clients. Additionally, I coordinated volunteers, childcare and donated catering from local vendors.

ADDITIONAL INTERNSHIP WORK

Created volunteer opportunities for volunteers to help prepare materials for events and outreach. o Created brochures, flyers, and other printed materials to be distributed at the DHR and food stamp offices to spread knowledge about domestic violence, sexual assault, and Turning Point. o Updated the Turning Point directory to optimize the efficiency and effectiveness of crisis calls o Created social media content for Turning Point Instagram account o Facilitated a collaboration fundraiser event between UA Print Club and Turning Point
